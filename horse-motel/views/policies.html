House & barn rules

  • Before you leave: gather used towels, throw trash away, turn things off, and lock up.

Stall cameras

Each stall has a camera so guests can check on their own horses. When you book a stall, you can watch only the camera(s) on the stall(s) assigned to you, from {{camBefore}} hours before check-in until {{camAfter}} hours after check-out. If one camera covers two neighbouring stalls, guests in either stall can see both while their stays overlap. Exterior security cameras also operate on the property.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, phone number and booking details to run your reservation and give you camera access. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never receive or store your card number. We don’t sell your information or use advertising trackers, and this site sets only one cookie — to keep you signed in.

For security we keep a log of sign-ins and camera views (time, account and IP address) for about a year. To see or delete your information, contact us.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to book and use this site. It is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA: it works with a keyboard alone, with screen readers, with zoom up to 400%, and in light, dark and high-contrast modes. The house is single-level.

If anything on the site gets in your way, please tell us and we’ll help you book directly and fix the problem.
