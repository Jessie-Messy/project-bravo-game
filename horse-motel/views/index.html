Questions

Frequently asked

Can I book stalls without the house?

Yes. Book any mix: stalls only, an RV or trailer hookup only, the house only, or all three.

How do the stall cameras work?

After you pay, you’ll get an email with a one-time link to create your password. Sign in on any phone, tablet or computer and your stall cameras appear under “My stay”. They switch on {{camBefore}} hours before check-in and turn off {{camAfter}} hours after check-out. You can only watch the cameras on the stalls you’ve rented. Tip: add the site to your phone’s home screen to open your cameras like an app.

What if I already have an account?

Book with the same email address and the new stay — and its cameras — will be added to your existing account automatically.

Google Maps can’t find you.

Google tends to get lost out here in the country. Use our exact coordinates: 34.863750° N, 91.916582° W — the directions links at the bottom of this page use them.
